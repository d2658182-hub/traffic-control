# Traffic Control

## About the game

You are the eyes and hands of an entire city's busiest crossroads. Cars roll in from every direction while trams barrel down their rails, and a single careless collision snarls everything. Tap a vehicle to give it a burst of speed, thread it through the intersection, and watch near-misses turn into chains of satisfying clears. Every level is a new hand-drawn junction with its own rhythm, shortcuts and traps.

The genius is in the tension: you can never move everything at once, so every tap is a tiny gamble. Slow down at the perfect moment, feel a tram whoosh past a bumper, and chain successful passes into medals and new bests. Pick-up-and-play in seconds, deceptively hard to put down — exactly the kind of game that turns "one more level" into an hour.

## Controls

### Smartphone & tablet

- Tap a car to speed it up and push it through the junction.
- Use the on-screen slow-down button to calm tricky situations.
- Tap the on-screen buttons to pause, restart or return to the menu.

### Desktop

- Left-click a car to speed it up.
- Left-click the slow-down button when the traffic gets dense.
- Left-click the pause / restart / home icons as needed.

> Trackpad: single click = same as left-click.
